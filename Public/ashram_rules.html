ISKCON TOKYO

ATITHI SEVA NIWAS (GUEST ASHRAM)
Guest Guidelines, Code of Spiritual Conduct & Daily Timings

1. The Four Regulative Principles (Strictly Mandatory)

All devotees, guests, and pilgrims staying at the ashram must strictly observe the four regulative principles of Vedic culture on and around the premises:

  • No Meat, Fish, or Eggs: Strict lacto-vegetarian Prasadam only. Outside non-vegetarian food, onion, garlic, or items with animal gelatin are prohibited.
  • No Intoxication: Smoking, tobacco, e-cigarettes/vapes, alcohol, or illicit substances are forbidden anywhere inside or outside the premises.
  • No Gambling: Wagering, speculative card games, and financial gambling are not permitted.
  • No Illicit Sex: Strict celibacy, modesty, and respectful moral decorum must be honored by all guests throughout their stay.

2. Ashram Etiquette & Decorum

  • Modest Clothing: Devotional attire (Dhoti/Kurta, Saree, Gopi skirts) or modest western clothing covering shoulders, chest, and knees.
  • Saucam (Cleanliness): Please leave street footwear in designated shoe racks upon entry. Keep your room and washrooms tidy.
  • Quiet Hours (22:00 – 05:00): Please observe silence during late hours to support fellow devotees in their morning sadhana.
  • Honoring Prasadam: Take only what you can eat. Devotees wash their own plates and cutlery after meals.

3. Room Accommodation Rules

  • Room Allocation: Matajis (women) stay in Room 201; Prabhujis (men) stay in Room 202.
  • Room Capacity: Strictly maximum 2 adults per room.
  • Energy Conservation: Please turn off all ACs, heaters, and lights when leaving the room.

5. Check-In / Check-Out

  • Check-in Window: 13:00 – 20:00 PM
  • Check-out: By 11:00 AM sharp
  • Valid ID Required: Passport / Japan Residence Card required at check-in.
  • Seva Contribution: Full balance payable in Cash (JPY ¥) upon arrival.
